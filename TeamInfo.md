## Project Summary

Flight Tracking Optimizer is a flight route analytics web application developed by The Foreign Key Club. The project helps travelers make more informed flight decisions by analyzing historical U.S. domestic flight data, including route reliability, airline performance, delay patterns, cancellation rates, and airfare trends.

Unlike traditional flight booking platforms that mainly focus on ticket availability and price, this application emphasizes risk analysis. Users can search for routes, compare airlines operating on the same route, view common delay causes, and evaluate the tradeoff between affordability and reliability before booking a trip.

The system combines multiple real-world aviation datasets, including data from the Department of Transportation, Bureau of Transportation Statistics, and OpenFlights. Our goal is to build a database-driven application that provides useful travel context for users planning high-stakes trips, such as business travel, international connections, or other time-sensitive travel plans.
